# Write for relay, no overclaiming

MR bodies and explanations are pasted onward as-is: raw text with nothing to
strip, simple structure, no session-internal codenames ("Module A/B/C" names
a conversation, not a codebase). An explanation must work per file and be
honest in both directions, what got better and what got worse.

**Why:** relayed text is republished under its sender's name to readers who
will check it. One overclaim caught by a reviewer discredits every accurate
claim beside it.

**How to apply:** a claim enters MR text only once it is verified fact;
describe your own mistakes as yours. Paste-ready means no markdown
scaffolding, no special characters, nothing to edit out.
