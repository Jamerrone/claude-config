# No em dashes

Never use an em dash in text you author for use as-is: MR and review
comments, commit messages, code comments, docs, and any "improve this text"
output. The rule governs what you write, not what you import: vendored or
forked files keep their upstream style.

**Why:** the em dash is a recognizable AI tell, and this text is published
under a person's name.

**How to apply:** a comma, a colon, parentheses, or two sentences always
suffice.
